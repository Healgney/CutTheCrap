## 1) Task definition
- [F] Hard prompt compression (token/sentence/chunk selection under a token budget, no rewriting). Claim: scoring units independently can keep a task-relevant unit while dropping the unit that defines its entities ("referential dangling"). Formal definition (Section 3): a retained sentence s with no complete sufficient support set in the output, support limited to what is explicitly stated in the source (no commonsense), query-dependent; formal definition restricted to extractive hard compressors. A proposition shows additive scoring objectives give no guarantee for arbitrary utilities (existence result only).
- [F] Analyses: prevalence (Beaver), six-compressor comparison, fixed-budget reselection (restore omitted supporting paragraph), and an automatic restoration pipeline (candidate generator + BERT-base ranker).

## 2) Labels / annotation source and agreement
- [F] No new human labels. Dangling is detected by a heuristic on existing multi-hop QA annotations (annotated supporting paragraphs; bridge questions with an "answer paragraph" and a "definition paragraph").
- [F] Manual audit: 40 flagged examples inspected; 38 confirmed (omitted paragraph contained the answer string in 26, the entity definition in 10, another dependency in 2); 2 false positives (answer paragraph self-sufficient; omitted paragraph supplied only a generic modifier). Annotator number and procedure are not described; no agreement statistic. No LLM-judge validation.
- [F] Restoration classifier labels (programmatic): positive = retained sentence and an omitted annotated supporting sentence sharing an entity (3,026 of 157,887 candidate pairs, 1.9%), hard negatives share an entity but not the rule, easy negatives share none; negatives downsampled to 1.5x positives (7,565 pairs, 40% positive).

## 3) How self-containedness (here: support completeness) is defined and measured
- [F] Definition = Section 3 (above). Measurement (Section 4): directional content-word coverage cov(v,A) = max over retained output units a of |CW(v) intersect CW(a)| / |CW(v)|; Keep_theta holds when cov >= theta (theta=0.5, swept 0.3-0.7 in Appendix A). Answer-path dangling = answer unit kept, paired definition unit not kept. rho_d = fraction of bridge examples with retained answer paragraph whose definition paragraph is not kept; rho_e = fraction of all examples retaining all evidence. The paper states this "approximates Equation (1) rather than evaluating it exactly" and does not enumerate alternative support paths.
- [F] LongBench-v2 SDQA (no support annotations): first-mention diagnostic -- a later reference dangles when the sentence of the entity first mention is omitted while the referring sentence survives. 80 documents, mean per-document rate 30.5%, all 80 have at least one; subdomain range 25-37%. Different denominator, not comparable with rho_d.
- [F] No correlation of the detector with human judgements beyond the 40-example audit (95% precision, 38/40).

## 4) Unit / boundary
- [F] Units are paragraphs (prevalence) or sentences (cross-compressor, classifier); token-level compressors are regrouped by source sentence. No spoken or temporal units.

## 5) Metrics and key numbers
- [F] Beaver (Qwen3-0.6B embeddings, 64-token pages, ratio 0.30), Table 1: rho_d 34.2% HotpotQA, 53.5% 2WikiMultiHopQA, 54.2% MuSiQue; rho_e 61.0% / 30.7% / 27.0%. Figure 2a: rho_d rises from 6.3% at ratio 0.70 to 53.5% at 0.20 (bootstrap CIs non-overlapping). Substituting GPT-2 embeddings changes rho_d by <=0.9 points.
- [F] Six compressors on the same 184 HotpotQA bridge examples (sentence-level Keep_0.5): 32.1% (Beaver) to 59.8% (LongLLMLingua); 5.4% dangle under all six, 95% under at least one; query-aware methods sit at both ends (so query-awareness does not explain it).
- [F] Fixed-budget reselection on dangling subsets (Qwen3-8B; HotpotQA n=80, 2Wiki n=72): +28.8 to +34.3 points over Base (p<1e-4); recovers 92% and 88% of the Base-to-Full-support gap on HotpotQA and 2Wiki; exceeds full support by 1.9 on MuSiQue. Full support on open models (HotpotQA n=200) +8.0 to +9.0; GPT-5.5 +8.8 on MuSiQue, no aggregate change on HotpotQA.
- [F] Automatic restoration (BERT-base ranker on [retained; candidate; question], K=3): +4.7 points on HotpotQA with first-mention candidates (ratio 0.30 to 0.31; p=0.022) but only +0.5 on 2Wiki; hybrid candidates +4.5 / +5.5; oracle-candidate diagnostic +8.0 (p=0.008); matched random addition +2.0 (contrast with targeted restoration not significant, p=0.15); gains on three other compressors not significant.

## 8) Limitations stated
- The detector is an approximation; restoration is a diagnostic not a complete compressor; configuration calibrated on Beaver + HotpotQA, transfer to other compressors and beyond annotated-support QA "remains unresolved"; the contrast between targeted and random restoration is suggestive but not significant at this sample size.

## 9) Differences from our direction, and can it run on ASR transcripts? (assignment (b), evidence only from this paper)
- Evidence available: every number is on written, paragraph-structured English Wikipedia/QA text (HotpotQA, 2Wiki, MuSiQue) or LongBench-v2 documents; nothing on speech, ASR noise, dialogue, or clips.
- Detector mechanics: needs (i) an external answer/support annotation for the diagnostic, or (ii) for the label-free first-mention diagnostic a notion of "entity first mention" and sentence segmentation. The restoration ranker needs a question and shared entities, and was trained on HotpotQA only (+4.7 on one dataset, +0.5 on another).
- Verdict [I]: the concept (relevance-only selection leaves referents behind; 30-60% rates; large QA losses when repaired) is directly relevant to clip selection, but nothing in the paper shows the detectors work on ASR text. The cheapest transferable piece is the first-mention diagnostic (entity mention in clip whose first mention in the episode lies outside the clip); its validity on ASR is untested, and it has no human validation beyond the 95% (38/40) audit of the paragraph-level flag.
- Other differences: query-conditioned QA utility instead of viewer engagement; token budget instead of 15-90 s time window; no boundary selection; preprint status.

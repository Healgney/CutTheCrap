# C17 Extractive is not Faithful (Zhang, Wan, Bansal; ACL 2023; arXiv 2209.03549)
reading_depth: sections 1-5 (ar5iv; last ~2.7k chars of page unread)

- [F] Five problem types: incorrect coreference (pronoun or determiner+noun refers to a different entity than in the document); incomplete coreference (anaphor with ambiguous or missing antecedent, e.g. a summary starting "That is why ..."); incorrect discourse; incomplete discourse (linking term such as "But" without its context); other misleading information.
- [F] Annotation: 100 CNN/DM test articles x 16 extractive systems (10 supervised, 6 unsupervised) = 1,600 summaries; 4 annotators (2 PhD authors, 2 CS undergraduates), ~3 months; each summary annotated by 2, disagreements resolved jointly; SpanBERT coref (AllenNLP) output shown to annotators; no kappa reported.
- [F] Rates: any problem 30.3% (484); incomplete coref 15.4%; incomplete discourse 10.7%; misleading 4.9%; incorrect coref 3.9%; incorrect discourse 1.1%. Oracle summaries worst, Lead3 best.
- [F] ExtEval = IncorCorefEval + IncomCorefEval (SpanBERT clusters, summary vs document) + IncomDiscoEval (linking terms with missing adjacent context) + SentiBias. Overall r=0.54 / rho=0.46 vs best existing metric (BERTScore-P) r=0.37; sub-metric r: IncomDisco 0.61, IncomCoref 0.48, IncorCoref 0.25. ~0.43 s/example on a TITAN V.
- [F] Repair check: of 32 flagged incorrect coreferences, 28 were true (87.5% precision), recall 46.7%; a simple program fixed 16 of 28.
- [F] Limitations: only CNN/DM (plus 23 PubMed oracle summaries checked by hand). Code/data: github.com/ZhangShiyue/extractive_is_not_faithful (license not stated; CNN/DM Apache-2.0 per footnote).

## Implication
- [I] "Incomplete coreference" (15.4%) and "incomplete discourse" (10.7%) is the closest published taxonomy to "dangling anaphora / missing setup". Detector logic (is the first mention in the extract a pronoun/determiner while the first mention in the full document is not?) can be applied to a clip vs its episode transcript. Not validated on ASR or spoken language.

# C06 From Text Segmentation to Smart Chaptering (YTSeg) (Retkowski, Waibel; EACL 2024; arXiv 2402.17633)
reading_depth: sections dataset/model/metrics/results via ar5iv

- [F] YTSeg: 19,299 English YouTube videos, 393 channels, ~6,533 content hours; creator-provided captions and chapters (not auto-generated); avg 20.3 min (SD 25.3), 196.2 sentences, 9.12 segments per document; 173,195 section-title pairs (YTSeg[Titles], titles avg 4.03 words). Chapters can start mid-sentence; sentences assigned by time overlap. CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 on Hugging Face.
- [F] MiniSeg: MiniLM sentence encoder (33M) + 12-layer RoFormer document encoder (26M) = ~59M; transcript-only; weighted BCE [1,2]; online variant with limited future context c.
- [F] Metrics: P/R/F1, Pk, Boundary Similarity (B); WindowDiff not reported.
- [F] Results: Wiki-727K F1 69.65, Pk 17.57 (vs 66.50 hierarchical BERT). YTSeg from scratch F1 43.37, Pk 28.73, B 35.74; with Wiki-727K pretraining F1 45.81, Pk 27.13, B 37.89. Online: F1 40.35 (c=0) -> 44.26 (c=8), plateau after 3-5 sentences. Removing weighted BCE: F1 38.39.
- [F] Chaptering titles (BART-large): ROUGE-1 42.79 with full context + previous titles vs 36.42 no context.
- [F] Audio/video are in the benchmark but unused in experiments.
- [I] RQ3: shows that sentence-level boundary classification on spoken-video transcripts is weak (F1 ~45) and ambiguous (creator chapters subjective); likely good as candidate-cut prior, not as ground truth for clip ends.

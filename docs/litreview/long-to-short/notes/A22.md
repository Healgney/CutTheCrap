# A22 Pharmacy video clips (arXiv 2601.05059) -- reading notes

Read: arXiv HTML v1: abstract, intro, related work, dataset/settings, method, results tables (Table 2-4), ablation on role/prompt, limitations. Source: https://arxiv.org/html/2601.05059v1

- [F] Industrial paper (Roche / Accenture / Involead authors; patent application filed per footnote; arXiv comment says under peer review). Task: long clinical-trial interviews / seminars / conference recordings -> highlight clips; pipeline = audio-language model transcription + Gemini 2.5 Pro text-to-text segment selection (with reasoning) + Cut & Merge (fade-in/out, timestamp normalisation); personalisation by role definition and prompt injection (marketing/training/regulatory); outputs vertical/horizontal with subtitles.
- [F] Data: Video-MME 900 videos (300 sampled long videos for clip metrics) and a proprietary set of 16,159 pharmacy videos in 14 disease areas (sampled >300 long videos, <2 min to >3 h, plus 888 audio). Proprietary data not released.
- [F] Metrics are embedding-based: "clip coherence" = fraction of original segments covered by any summary clip; informativeness = mean cosine similarity of summary clip to its best-matching original segment; redundancy = mean pairwise cosine among selected segments (Table 4, tau = 0.6). Reported: coherence 0.348, informativeness 0.721, redundancy 0.339 for their method; Gemini 2.5 Pro has best "logical flow" but is slower/more expensive; Gemini 2.5 Flash returns many fragmented segments (e.g. 85 segments for one video).
- [F] Efficiency: using ALM transcript + text-only Gemini instead of full-video Gemini gives 3-4x speed-up and 3.5-4x cost reduction (30-55 s per video).
- [I] "Coherence" here is a coverage statistic, not a measure of self-containedness; no human validation; no engagement notion. Included only because it is another LLM-over-transcript long-speech-to-clips system with reportable (but weak) evidence.

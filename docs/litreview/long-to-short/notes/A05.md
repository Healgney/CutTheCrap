## Dataset construction
- [F] Label = YouTube "Most replayed" statistic (public for select videos) treated as proxy for highlight score. Provided as 100 uniformly spaced normalised scores per video (e.g. 6 s bins for a 600 s video), aligned to YouTube-8M 1 fps features.
- [F] Filters: videos with >=50,000 views ("so that importance is not biased towards a few users"); deleted videos dropped; videos >300 s excluded (YT8M features only up to 300 s, and cropping would damage relative importance); music category excluded ("significant changes in Most replayed depending on the audio" while task focuses on visual cues).
- [F] Result: 31,892 videos, 1,788 h, duration 121-300 s (mean 201.9 s), views 50k-290M, 3,509 YT8M entity categories (153 with >=100 videos).
- [F] Features are YouTube-8M Inception-v3 PCA-1024 at 1 fps only (visual); no audio, no transcript.

## Tasks and metrics
- [F] Highlight detection: 5-s shots, average of frame scores; top 15% and 50% = GT; metric MAP. Summarisation: KTS shot boundaries + 0/1 knapsack with 15% budget; metric F1 (Zhang et al. protocol).
- [F] 2,000-video test set; PGL-SUM F1 55.89, MAP50 61.60, MAP15 27.45; VASNet 55.26/58.69/25.28; SL-module 55.31/58.63/24.95; DSNet 50.78/57.31/24.35; iPTNet 50.53/55.53/22.74.
- [F] Validation-size study: tens of videos give unstable F1; >=1,000 videos needed for robust validation. TVSum 5-fold CV for PGL-SUM varies from 49.0 to 60.7 F1 across splits/seeds.

## Reliability checks of replay labels
- [F] Cross-dataset transfer: models trained on Mr.HiSum (22,743 realistic videos) and tested on TVSum/YouTube Highlights/SumMe. SL-module MAP: TVSum target 71.3, zero-shot 69.5, fine-tuned 73.0; YouTube Highlights target 59.9, zero-shot 57.9, fine-tuned 62.0. PGL-SUM F1 TVSum 55.5 -> 57.1; SumMe 41.7 -> 42.3 (Mr.HiSum-trained).
- [F] User study: 25 videos x 10 candidate 5-10 s clips (5 highlight / 5 not per Mr.HiSum), 30 annotators, 6 votes/clip. Agree 64.8% (23.6% 6:0, 24.4% 5:1, 16.8% 4:2), neutral 15.6%, disagree 19.6%.

## Known biases admitted by authors
- [F] "Self-fulfilling bias": the Most-Replayed UI appears only after >=50k views and users may replay what others already replayed; cannot be removed because YouTube does not release stats for the first 50k viewers. Authors argue impact small because it is explicit replays.
- [F] Replay counting: unclear how partial rewatches are counted (YouTube does not disclose); authors argue only adjacent segments affected.
- [I] Additional biases not discussed: popularity/selection (>=50k views, YT8M sampling, only videos where the feature is enabled), coarse 100-bin resolution (3 s bins at 300 s, ~36 s bins at 1 h, so useless for second-level boundaries on long videos), seek-bar UI position/scrubbing behaviour (skip-back to start, intro replay) not modelled.

# D03 Agarwal, Zaitsev, Wang, Li, Najork, Joachims - Estimating Position Bias without Intrusive Interventions (WSDM 2019; arXiv 1812.05161)
Reading depth: sections 1, 3.1-3.4, 4 (skim), 5.1-5.3 from PDF text (cached copy).

- Claim [F]: first consistent propensity estimator under the position-based model without manual relevance labels, disruptive interventions or relevance-model assumptions.
- Intervention Harvesting [F, 3.3]: use click logs from m historic rankers; interventional set S_{k,k'} = (q,d) pairs where one ranker puts d at k and another at k'. Crucial condition (Eq. 1): the query distribution must not depend on the ranker, Pr(Q|f_i)=Pr(Q), i.e. A/B randomisation or negligible temporal shift. Weights w(q,d,k) correct non-uniform assignment.
- Estimators [F, 4]: local estimators (AdjacentChain, PivotOne) vs global AllPairs extremum estimator; AllPairs needs about an order of magnitude less data than AdjacentChain (5.3.2).
- Real data [F, 5.1-5.2]: arXiv full-text search, 53,000 queries / 25,600 clicks (May 14-Aug 1 2018), half used for a gold-standard swap experiment; AllPairs lies mostly within the swap-experiment 95% CI with a tighter CI. Also Google Drive search.
- Robustness [F, 5.3 Yahoo LTR simulation]: recovers true propensity; robust to click noise (not confounded by rank), imbalanced ranker traffic; error rises when rankers are too similar or too dissimilar, and sharply when the propensity curve is steep.
- For us [I]: transferable only if the same content appears at several timeline positions under quasi-random assignment; a video analogue would be the same speech segment in different edits/uploads. Otherwise use the factor-model route (D02).

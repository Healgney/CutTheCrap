# C15 Bridging Context Gaps (Mohanty, Xuan, Jobraeel, Kumar, Roy, Kabbara; COLING 2025; arXiv 2412.19966)
reading_depth: sections abstract, 1, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 9 (HTML). Relation: nearest NLP work that measures what an excerpt from a long conversation loses outside its source.

- [F] Setting: small-group facilitated conversations (Fora corpus, Schroeder et al. 2024): 262 conversations, avg 66 min, median 6 participants, avg 152 turns, ~7k-12.5k words.
- [F] HSE dataset: 1,774 human-highlighted salient excerpts over 177 conversations; avg 111 words; >75% carry topic tags. Crowd (Prolific) annotated speaker demographics / motivations / locations / events for 100 excerpts from 35 conversations (~90 kept after attention filtering; 4-5 annotators each).
- [F] Formalization: enriched excerpt e~ = f(C, e), C = full conversation, e = excerpt, f = LLM. The paper gives no formal definition of "context gap".
- [F] Methods: CEE_i (implicit, zero-shot) and CEE_e (explicit, in-context examples asking for speaker characteristics, motivation, events/locations, term clarification) with GPT-4o; also Claude Opus and Llama-3.1-70B (explicit only).
- [F] Human eval: 75 Prolific raters, >=3 per excerpt, 1-5 Likert on understandability, readability, redundancy, completeness, cohesiveness + speaker-perception items. Table 1 (433 responses): understandability original 3.43 -> CEE_i 4.11 / CEE_e 4.13; completeness 3.07 -> 3.96 / 3.83; Welch t-test p<0.05. Lengths: original ~128 words, CEE_i ~267, CEE_e ~55.
- [F] Faithfulness (GPT-4o extraction vs HSE annotations, F1): good on short factors (name, gender, race), weak on long-form factors (speaker background 0.43 / 0.69 and location 0.51 / 0.34 for CEE_i / CEE_e); Claude most faithful.
- [F] Term clarification: of 128 flagged responses needing term explanations, CEE_e addressed only 55.
- [F] Summarization use (50 conversations, 156 ratings, 34 raters): summaries from context-enriched excerpts best on coherence 4.19, informativeness 4.26, detail 4.16; excerpt-only best on readability 4.25.
- [F] Limitations stated: only 90 excerpts evaluated (cost); temperature 0; open-ended F1 depressed by annotator disagreement. Data: GitHub (shresh02/bridging_context_gaps) per paper; license [V].

## Difference from our setting
- [F] Excerpts are human-picked salient passages in TEXT; the method repairs them by adding context rather than selecting excerpts for self-containedness; no audio or video; no clip boundaries; no engagement signal.
- [I] Raw understandability of 3.43/5 on human-chosen "salient" excerpts supports the assumption that salience does not imply self-containedness.

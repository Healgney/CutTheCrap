# C05 PODTILE (Ghazimatin, Garmash, Penha, et al.; CIKM 2024 industry; arXiv 2410.16148)
reading_depth: sections task/data/model/metrics/results via HTML

- [F] Task: chapterization = divide an episode transcript into "semantically coherent, non-overlapping segments" with titles, jointly.
- [F] Data: ~10.8k internal Spotify English episodes with creator chapters (train/val/test ~8.8k/1.1k/1.1k); avg transcript ~16k tokens, ~11.3 chapters; chapters 30 s to 30 min. Public transfer sets: WikiSection (23,129 docs), QMSum (232).
- [F] Model: LongT5-base (~220M) fine-tuned, "Gen(seg+label)", chunks of up to 8,000 words processed left to right (avg 1.75 chunks), with static context (title, description) and dynamic context (previous chapter titles), up to 1,000 words of the 8,000.
- [F] Metrics: WindowDiff; aligned ROUGE-L F1 and SBERT F1 for titles.
- [F] Offline: podcast ROUGE-L F1 0.231 vs 0.208 (about 11% relative); WindowDiff 0.365 vs 0.364 (no gain on boundaries); Wikisection WindowDiff 0.134 vs 0.188; longer docs gain more (12.08% vs 6.61%).
- [F] Online: limited roll-out from April 2024 (not an A/B test): +88.12% chapter-initiated plays; engagement ratio of auto vs creator chapters ~0.75 (less popular shows) vs ~0.53 (popular).
- [F] Text-only (audio future work). No claim of self-containedness; acknowledges chapterization is subjective and a single reference may not capture model quality.
